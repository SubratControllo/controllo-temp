# Controllo live route inventory and React overlap

Date reviewed: 2026-08-29

Scope: the current public WordPress site at `controllo.ai`, its robots file, sitemap index and child sitemaps, rendered homepage navigation/footer, public WordPress REST metadata, and representative route responses. This note is evidence only; it does not choose the future information architecture.

## Inventory summary

- The sitemap index exposes **259 URLs**: 188 pages, 39 posts, 13 category archives, 3 ElementsKit templates, and 16 form endpoints. [Sitemap index](https://controllo.ai/sitemap_index.xml), [page sitemap](https://controllo.ai/page-sitemap.xml), [post sitemap](https://controllo.ai/post-sitemap.xml), [category sitemap](https://controllo.ai/category-sitemap.xml), [template sitemap](https://controllo.ai/elementskit_template-sitemap.xml), [form sitemap](https://controllo.ai/metform-form-sitemap.xml)
- Public page bodies are mostly long: 174 of 188 pages have at least 500 body words in the public REST record, 3 have 120–499 words, and 11 have fewer than 120. Length is not a quality judgment; many long pages sit in obvious topical-overlap families.
- The sitemap includes internal implementation artifacts: three header/footer template query URLs and 16 MetForm form routes. It also includes `/test/` and `/elementor-13431/`, both accessible as HTTP 200 pages with self-referencing canonicals during inspection. [test page](https://controllo.ai/test/), [Elementor page](https://controllo.ai/elementor-13431/)
- Ten page-title pairs repeat exactly in public metadata: HIPAA, HITRUST, Colorado Privacy Act, ISO 27701, GDPR, Risk Management, Utah AI Act, EU AI Act, Colorado AI Act, and CIS. The risk pair is separately self-canonical rather than consolidated. [risk-management](https://controllo.ai/risk-management/), [risk-management-2](https://controllo.ai/risk-management-2/)
- The largest URL-topic families are privacy/data protection, AI law/governance, ISO 27001, SOC 2, GRC/compliance automation, PCI DSS, NIST, CCM, and cloud/asset monitoring. The appendix identifies the family on every applicable route.
- The React application exposes 24 concrete routes from its router and content data, while its checked-in sitemap lists 18. The React sitemap currently omits the three resource detail URLs and the `/privacy`, `/terms`, and `/accessibility` routes that the router exposes.

## Redirect, canonical, TLS, and crawler observations

- `http://controllo.ai/` redirects to `https://controllo.ai/`, and HSTS is present on HTTPS responses.
- `https://controllo.ai/sitemap.xml` redirects to `https://controllo.ai/sitemap_index.xml`. The robots file points at the latter. [robots.txt](https://controllo.ai/robots.txt), [sitemap index](https://controllo.ai/sitemap_index.xml)
- `https://www.controllo.ai/` fails normal TLS hostname verification because the served certificate does not include the `www.controllo.ai` hostname. The canonical apex host is accessible.
- `/privacy/` and `/privacy-policy/` are separate, accessible, self-canonical pages with different intent: the former is product/privacy-operations content; the latter is the legal privacy policy. [privacy product](https://controllo.ai/privacy/), [privacy policy](https://controllo.ai/privacy-policy/)
- `/risk-management/` and `/risk-management-2/` both have the title `Risk Management - Controllo` and each self-canonicalizes instead of redirecting to one URL. [risk-management](https://controllo.ai/risk-management/), [risk-management-2](https://controllo.ai/risk-management-2/)
- A parallel HTTP pass found normal 200 responses until the WordPress host began returning 429 responses under concurrency. Appendix rows distinguish observed 200s, rate-limited checks, and incomplete individual checks; a 429 from this scan is not classified as a broken route.

## Classification method

- `Substantive by body length`: at least 500 words in the route body returned by the public WordPress REST API.
- `Moderate`: 120–499 body words. `Thin`: fewer than 120.
- `Overlap family`: the slug belongs to a repeated, observable topic family. This flags collision risk; it does not assert plagiarism or exact duplicate body copy.
- `Published`: listed in a public Rank Math sitemap, and when available also present as `publish` in the public WordPress REST API.
- HTTP statuses are observations from this review, not uptime guarantees. Rate-limit responses are recorded without treating the underlying page as unavailable.
